## Truth and accuracy policy

1. Never invent business facts, professional qualifications, credentials, awards, certifications, testimonials, project details, customer outcomes, legal approvals, or performance statistics.
2. Distinguish personal experience from team/company experience.
3. Use only supplied or reliably verified property information.
4. When a fact is unknown, leave it out, label it as unverified, or request confirmation.
5. Never promise guaranteed appreciation, investment returns, legal safety, loan approval, project completion, or possession.
6. Do not imply that RERA registration guarantees a project's quality, completion, title, or financial performance.
7. Do not claim legal title or every document has been independently verified unless the relevant verification actually took place.
8. Never generate fake first-hand experience, fake author biographies, invented experts, or fabricated customer reviews.

## E-E-A-T standards

Experience:
- Explain real business processes, relevant experience, and practical guidance.
- Do not manufacture first-hand experience for SEO purposes.

Expertise:
- Explain property terminology, buying considerations, and verification steps accurately.
- Use qualifications only when confirmed.
- Recommend qualified legal, financial, or technical professionals for matters outside the business's verified scope.

Authoritativeness:
- Link to genuine business profiles, official records, relevant sources, and real authors where available.
- Build authority through useful, accurate, maintained content rather than self-awarded labels such as "No. 1" or "most trusted."

Trustworthiness:
- Make business identity, contact information, content responsibility, and limitations clear.
- Explain how property information is sourced or checked.
- Provide a correction/contact mechanism.
- Encourage independent due diligence before a property transaction.

## Content and SEO rules

- Write for users first, search engines second.
- Use descriptive titles, helpful meta descriptions, canonical URLs, logical headings, and meaningful internal links.
- Do not force arbitrary word counts or stuff keywords into content.
- Do not publish thin or near-duplicate location, builder, project, or property pages.
- Use structured data only when relevant and consistent with visible page content.
- Keep published facts, structured data, metadata, and page content consistent.
- Display review or update dates only when supported by real review or update activity.
- Never use schema to claim ratings, credentials, or reviews that are not visibly and genuinely supported.

## Property information and verification

- Check RERA registration where applicable.
- Review available developer information and project documents.
- Clearly distinguish verified facts from information supplied by a seller, developer, or other source.
- Encourage independent checks of title, approvals, agreements, payment terms, and other relevant documents.
- State when pricing, availability, possession, or other information may change.
- Do not describe every resale property or property category as necessarily requiring the same RERA checks.

## Privacy and lead generation

- Collect only information necessary for the stated enquiry.
- Keep private contact details and lead information out of public pages, sitemaps, metadata, structured data, and public APIs.
- Do not send names, phone numbers, email addresses, or other personal data to analytics events.
- Use clear, non-deceptive lead forms and explain why information is requested.
